**Presenter:** Fatemeh (Ghazal) Moradihaghighi

**Event:** TraCR Scholar Webinar Series

**Date:** October 6, 2026

This talk presents Fuzz'EMup, an approach to black-box embedded firmware fuzzing that leverages electromagnetic (EM) side-channel emanations as execution feedback. It discusses how physical signals can guide security testing when conventional software instrumentation and code-coverage feedback are unavailable.
